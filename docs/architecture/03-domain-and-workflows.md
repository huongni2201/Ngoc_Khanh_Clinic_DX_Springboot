# Domain model and supported workflows

## Domain design

Aggregates own consistency boundaries and mutation methods. Entities retain
identity; value objects are immutable. Creation and restoration enforce
invariants. Preserve separate request, command/query, domain, persistence and
integration models where their responsibilities differ. Stable contracts are
not Map<String,Object>. Use typed staging payloads serialized only in adapters.

Pass Clock-derived instants from application into domain operations. Use UUIDv7
when allocating persisted identity; exact CCCD is the mandatory patient identity.
Patient/clinical/financial history is not hard-deleted. No fuzzy identity merge
or age-eligibility rejection is part of the implemented roster/record workflow.

### Patient identity

CCCD uses the technical field `identification_number`, stored as text. Exact lookup
precedes Patient creation; `patients.identification_number` is unique. The current
baseline has no passport/identity-type abstraction or fuzzy merge by name/phone.
Patient contacts/addresses/merge-history tables require an explicit new contract.

## Account authentication

Account owns username/password_hash and exactly one STAFF or PATIENT owner.
StaffMember is clinic staff, separate from an organization Participant. An account
holds exactly one role, with no invented assignment IDs, time windows or location
scopes; the PATIENT role belongs to patient accounts and every other role to staff
accounts. Login requires active account, valid credentials and a valid owner; an
inactive role grants nothing. Authentication itself does not grant clinical or
portal access.

Username is case-sensitive and is not trimmed. Passwords are not trimmed, but
are NFKC-normalized before bcrypt verification; the 72-byte limit applies after
normalization. The session protocol is described in
[ADR-0014](../adr/0014-session-cookie-redis-login.md); refresh-token storage
fields do not invent a new endpoint.

## Organization and Agreement

Organization has an optional unique tax code, general contact channels and one
named contact; general phone is optional. It has no separate business code. An Agreement
(Thỏa thuận, [ADR-0019](../adr/0019-agreement-session-assignment.md)) owns an ordered set of
service lines with negotiated prices and an optional validity period. Its states are DRAFT,
ACTIVE and ENDED. It has no day, site or attendance: those belong to sessions. Draft
configuration updates preserve retained service line IDs and reject removal of referenced
lines; only a DRAFT agreement can be edited, services included. Once it is ACTIVE the services
are fixed. A service has at most one active line; a line retired earlier stays as history.

The agreement code is generated by the system on create (`KSK-<year>-<running number>`,
for example `KSK-2026-001`). The year follows Asia/Ho_Chi_Minh; numbering uses
the highest used suffix for that year, including deleted agreements, under a
transaction-scoped advisory lock. Numbering starts again for a new year.
Create/update requests omit the code; an extra `agreementCode` is ignored by the
current transport contract. An update preserves the stored code.

Agreement "delete" is a soft delete (`deleted_at`): only a DRAFT agreement with no Participant
(any roster status), no session and no import history can be deleted. The row, its lines and
its unique `agreement_code` are kept, and a deleted agreement is invisible to get,
list, update and further delete. Participant writes cannot touch a deleted agreement.
Import history is asked through the integration module's `AgreementHistoryQuery`.

Organization "delete" is deactivation: `ACTIVE` to `INACTIVE` through
`Organization.deactivate()`, guarded by the expected row version and audited in the same
transaction. The row and its agreements are preserved (agreement FK is `ON DELETE RESTRICT`) and
agreement workflows are unchanged. Inactive organizations are hidden from organization list
and get; updating one is not blocked by this workflow.

On service addition, capture catalog reference price; negotiated price belongs
to the Agreement. Ordinary edits preserve existing performed-item price snapshots.
Retroactive repricing is a separate authorized, audited business operation.

## Participant

Participant is an agreement-scoped identity/employment snapshot (it also carries the
organization), not an organization master aggregate or automatically a Patient. Roster,
scheduling, attendance and service reconciliation are independent:

- roster: ACTIVE/CANCELLED;
- scheduling and attendance: the Participant's session assignments (below); the attendance
  shown on screens is derived, not stored;
- reconciliation: PENDING/RECONCILED; zero items is meaningful only after reconciliation.

The roster retains optional identification issue date/place, ethnicity, address,
workplace and note. The system generates Participant codes on creation as
`<agreement code>-<4-digit running number>`, counting existing rows including cancelled
Participants; updates preserve the code.

Roster addition must never create Patient or Encounter. Authorized visit
preparation links or creates Patient by exact CCCD and prepares the visit. Moving a
Participant to another session preserves prepared links and attended history. Sensitive
changes use expected versions and audit in the application transaction.

ParticipantService records staff reconciliation of actual performed items within
agreement scope; it is independent of Doctor orders. New performed items use active
agreement services and their negotiated price. Existing identity, historical price
and established ServiceRequest links cannot change through reconciliation. Keep
unchecked rows with is_performed=false; save header/items atomically with versions.

## Session and assignment

A Session (Lịch khám) is a date, an activity (`ALL`, `SAMPLING`, `EXAMINATION`) and a site
(`CLINIC` or `ORGANIZATION_SITE` with an address) of one organization. It has no capacity and
no shifts, and it may be reserved to one agreement or shared by the agreements of the
organization. There is at most one OPEN session per organization, date, site and activity;
cancelling frees the slot, and a session with people assigned cannot be cancelled. The
contract is [sessions and assignments](../api/sessions-and-assignments.md).

An Assignment puts a Participant in a session. Its status is ASSIGNED, ATTENDED, NO_SHOW,
MOVED or CANCELLED. A Participant is live in a session (ASSIGNED or ATTENDED) at most once,
enforced by a partial unique index; a database trigger checks that the session serves the
Participant's agreement and organization. Moving inserts the new assignment first and marks
the old one MOVED pointing at it; sessions are locked in identifier order so concurrent
moves cannot deadlock. Attendance can be recorded only on or after the session date
(Asia/Ho_Chi_Minh), and a recorded attendance can be corrected on retry.

Derived attendance: ATTENDED when any assignment attended; NO_SHOW when one was missed and none
is still expected; otherwise UNCONFIRMED. A Participant with no live assignment is
"unscheduled". Cancelling a Participant releases its expected assignments and is refused
once it attended.

## Participant manual changes

Owner decision 2026-10-07; the HTTP contract is
[participant manual add, edit, cancel and reactivate](../api/participant-manual-crud.md).

- One Participant is added, edited or cancelled by hand in a DRAFT/ACTIVE agreement of an ACTIVE
  organization. Cancelling sets roster CANCELLED and releases expected assignments; there is no
  physical delete. A cancelled Participant is reactivated (CANCELLED → ACTIVE) on the same row,
  never re-added, and is scheduled again with a new assignment.
- The `(agreement_id, identification_number)` rule covers every roster status, so a cancelled
  Participant keeps its CCCD reserved.
- The CCCD is locked once `patient_id` is set. A Participant that was prepared, attended or
  reconciled cannot be cancelled, and such a row cannot be reactivated either (defensive guard).
  Reactivation changes only the roster status; reconciliation and import provenance are kept.
- Changes use the Participant `rowVersion`, lock agreement then Participant, and audit in the same
  transaction without personal data. The agreement `rowVersion` does not change.

## Participant Excel import and list

The supported roster import is a single-step flow for one Organization/Agreement,
with template download and list. Preview/confirm/cancel endpoints are absent.
The HTTP contract is
[participant import and list](../api/participant-import-and-list.md).

- Add-only: existing Participants are never updated or cancelled. A CCCD already in the
  agreement (any roster status) is rejected.
- All-or-nothing in one transaction: agreement lock, organization lookup, idempotency
  reserve or replay, then state/expected `rowVersion` checks for a new import,
  dedupe, VALIDATED import job and rows,
  Participant inserts in chunks, job confirmed, key completed, audit.
- Only DRAFT/ACTIVE agreements of an ACTIVE organization accept a new import. Imported rows are unscheduled: sessions are assigned afterwards. A completed
  identical request replays its receipt before lifecycle/version checks.
- The list masks the CCCD; the full number is never returned and never logged.
- Existing participant provenance and import history remain in the schema.

## Record history and other contexts

HealthExaminationRecord is one visit root with stable mrn/SHS shared across forms.
Its PREPARED/IN_PROGRESS/COMPLETED/ISSUED/CANCELLED lifecycle is separate from the
Agreement. Administrative snapshots and clinical record versions have typed separate
tables; final/issued content is immutable and corrections retain history.

Clinical assessments and diagnostics results have separate version roots.
Prescriptions have logical roots, official versions and version items. Billing
ServiceAuthorization gates service execution; each Encounter can contain multiple
OrderRounds. Encounter, ServiceRequest and Result retain their own lifecycles;
derive diagnostic progress and Doctor worklists from those records, OrderRound,
ServiceAuthorization and performing location. Doctor review readiness follows
completion of required requests/results; no reception/exam/return queue tickets
are introduced. Doctor creates orders; Front Desk owns baseline collection.
Billable diagnostics execute only with the documented payment authorization or
explicit exemption. Financial history is preserved.

Final results and issued prescriptions are versioned/corrected, not overwritten
or hard-deleted. Finalization enforces permissions; results remain traceable to
their ServiceRequest/Encounter, with explicit partial/final states. Attachments
do not replace structured results where structured data exists. Inventory and
dispensing are outside the current baseline.

Read current status/constraints from the clean-slate schema; do not
recreate a parallel queue model.

Portal releases reference exact official result/document versions and remain
independent of SMS/email outcomes. Document renders from source plus template
versions; issued representations retain those references. Backend render audit
records DOCUMENT_RENDERED, not proof that a browser print dialog printed paper.
Notification/outbox use deduplication and leases for durable post-commit delivery.
These storage contracts do not claim unsupported workflows have public endpoints.

## Examination details and payment report

A Participant's performed services are `HealthExaminationParticipantService` rows (price snapshot per row).
`reconcileServices` keeps existing rows when a service is unchecked (`is_performed=false`), adds new rows only for
performed active services at the negotiated price, and moves the Participant to RECONCILED. Reconciliation does not
write attendance. "Pending reconciliation" means derived ATTENDED and PENDING. The screen status labels are derived in
the frontend; the backend adds no parallel state machine. The payment report groups performed rows of the ACTIVE
roster by (agreement service, price snapshot), `amount = unit price × count`, may be limited to a session date range,
reports sessions and visits (attended assignments), and is provisional while the agreement is not ENDED. Business
"today" is Asia/Ho_Chi_Minh. See [examination details and report](../api/examination-details-and-report.md).
